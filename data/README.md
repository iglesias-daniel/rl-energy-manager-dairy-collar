# Data

| Folder | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `weather/explorador_solar_maquehue_hourly_2004-2016.csv` | Hourly radiation (global, direct, diffuse, DNI…), temperature and wind for Predio Maquehue, 2004–2016 | [Explorador Solar](https://solar.minenergia.cl/exploracion), Ministerio de Energía de Chile |
| `weather/explorador_solar_report.pdf` | Report generated by Explorador Solar for the site | Explorador Solar |
| `weather/SP_Maquehue_2023_vector.mat` | Hourly Sun position unit vectors (East, North, Zenith) for Maquehue | Generated with `matlab/common/sun-position/SP_Maquehue_gen.m` |
| `orientation/magne_aceler_better.mat` | `g_30dias`, `m_30dias`: 30 days of processed collar orientation (accelerometer / magnetometer) from 3 dairy cows, April 2024 | Monitoring collars, Predio Maquehue, Universidad de La Frontera |
| `solar-harvest-logs/*.txt` | Voltage/current of the solar panel (`voltage1`, `current1`) and battery (`voltage2`, `current2`) every ~5 min, Nov–Dec 2024. The file name is the download date and time | Prototype measurements |
| `power-measurements/joulescope_exp3_7v.xlsx` | Power profile of the device tasks at 3.7 V, used for the consumption model (Table V of the paper) | Joulescope JS220 |

The weather data is used under the terms of Explorador Solar. Cite the
Ministerio de Energía de Chile if you reuse it. The rest of the data in this
folder is released under CC BY 4.0 (citation of the article required, see `../LICENSE`).

The raw IMU recordings from the collars and the LoRaWAN uplinks from the
field deployment are not published here.
